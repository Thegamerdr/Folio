# Folio 10/10 Language System

Date: 2026-06-24

## Voice

Folio sounds calm, plain, specific and quietly reassuring. It does not dramatise money problems and does not hide behind technical language.

Use short, concrete sentences:

- "Rows wait for review before they are added."
- "Nothing changes your picture until you accept it."
- "You can see what has to last until payday."
- "Debt payments are shown as commitments, not judgments."
- "Show why."

## Preferred Words

- your picture
- what needs checking
- added
- ignored
- saved
- coming up
- what changed
- make it to payday
- organise debts
- check bills
- try fake data
- show why
- waiting rows
- breathing room
- must-pay item

## Avoid In Visible Copy

- canonical
- parser
- provenance
- financial reality
- make real
- event graph
- object count
- diagnostic
- AI detected
- confidence score
- scenario when abstract
- route when abstract

## Emotional Rules

- No shame language.
- No advice language.
- No fake certainty.
- No inflated reassurance.
- No childish metaphors.
- No corporate filler.
- No architecture terms in user-facing screens.
- Bad months use truth, context and a path forward.

## Melo Language

Melo can say what it noticed, why it matters and what the user controls. Melo must not imply authority over records or financial decisions.

Allowed:

- "I found rows that need your eye."
- "This can stay waiting until you decide."
- "This looks like a debt payment, but it needs your review."

Not allowed:

- "You should pay this first."
- "I fixed your finances."
- "AI detected this with high confidence."

## Review Language

Review copy must answer:

- What did Folio find?
- What might this be?
- What happens if I add it?
- What can I do with it?

Use action names that match user intent: Accept, Edit, Reject, Duplicate, Transfer, Income, Bill, Debt payment, Refund, Later.
