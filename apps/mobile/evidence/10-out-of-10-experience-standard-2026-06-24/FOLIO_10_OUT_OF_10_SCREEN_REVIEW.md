## Start

Purpose: help the user choose why they came and take the best first action.

Strengths: four clean data actions already existed.

Weakness before pass: action-first rather than mental-model-first.

Change: added lenses: Make it to payday, Organise debts, Check bills, Add bank activity, See where I stand, Guide me. The selected lens changes first question, route focus, review focus and recommended action.

## Review

Purpose: control what affects the money picture.

Strengths: review-before-save invariant already existed.

Weakness before pass: core accept/edit/reject actions existed but debt/bill/income/refund/later meanings were not prominent enough.

Change: added visible meaning actions and row-level labels for Income, Bill, Debt payment, Refund and Later. Added manual extraction paths for review-only files.

## Quick Estimate

Purpose: create the first useful picture without a spreadsheet.

Strengths: only asked for money now, next income and one payment.

Weakness before pass: looked like stacked fields.

Change: rebuilt as guided question cards with why text, estimate controls, skip controls, debt/bill language and immediate consequence preview.

## Today

Purpose: show where the user stands after reviewed data exists.

Decision: keep. Continue to ensure review rows are not silently counted and every major number remains inspectable.

## Timeline

Decision: demote. Good as an explanation surface, too advanced as a first destination.

## Calendar
