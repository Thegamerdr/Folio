# Accessibility Audit Foundation

Canonical source: `ACCESSIBILITY_AUDIT_FOUNDATION.md`.

Evidence summary:

- Source-level accessibility risks are documented.
- Touch target, text scaling, contrast, screen reader, disabled-state, reduced-motion, keyboard/input, scroll and bottom-nav safe-area risks are tracked.
- Full accessibility compliance is not claimed.
- Android TalkBack, large text and reduced-motion evidence remain required.
- iOS VoiceOver remains blocked until macOS/Xcode or EAS path exists.
