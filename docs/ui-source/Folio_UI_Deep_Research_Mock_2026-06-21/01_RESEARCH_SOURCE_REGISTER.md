# Research Source Register

Accessed 21 June 2026.

## Direct user communities

### r/YNAB
Observed themes:
- automatic categorisation becomes dangerous when it looks indistinguishable from reviewed data;
- “permission to spend” produces stronger emotional value than restriction;
- long setup and a difficult mental model discourage new users;
- mobile redesigns that hide routine fields create anger despite cleaner visuals;
- concrete progress and financial awareness can turn avoidance into control;
- users with unstable income worry about false security and need certainty labels;
- recovery is easier when users continue from the current position rather than “starting over.”

Representative discussions:
- Feature request: global toggle for automatically categorising payees
- YNAB Magic
- Six things learned in six months
- How to Get Started
- Please stop changing the mobile transaction interface
- What if you can’t climb out
- Recently divorced after financial abuse and coercive control

### r/MonarchMoney
Observed themes:
- silent data mutations destroy trust in the entire record;
- users want activity logs, original merchant strings and reversible automation;
- incorrect AI merchant naming can cause genuine fraud panic;
- a spending-guess game can teach self-awareness, but peer comparisons become contextless or creepy;
- optional novelty should be easy to dismiss and must not replace the app’s core explanatory job.

Representative discussions:
- Category transactions changed on their own
- Automatic merchant names getting worse
- Love the Guess Your Spend game

### r/povertyfinance and r/debtfree
Observed themes:
- surviving a month can itself be a meaningful milestone;
- financial stress is often tied to shame and fear of judgment;
- unstable life conditions make conventional long-term advice feel detached from reality;
- unexpected expenses near a debt milestone create burnout, not simple “non-compliance”;
- users need to see what remains safe and how recovery works.

Representative discussions:
- Made it through another month
- Feeling like a failure
- What point does “it gets better” stop being true?
- Unexpected expense during debt payoff

### r/Finch
Observed themes:
- a safe-space ritual loses trust when promotions enter it;
- adult users can find overly childish story language patronising;
- an “optional” quest still creates pressure when it blocks visual completion;
- forced social quests feel like growth marketing rather than care;
- users need skip, substitute and preference controls.

Representative discussions:
- Can we stop “add a friend” daily quests
- Community responses to promotional content and adult tone

### r/Duolingo
Observed themes:
- visible progress and ritual are powerful;
- brittle streaks can punish people during illness, bereavement or crisis;
- insensitive automated notifications become memorable trust failures;
- gamified products still need human support and recovery mechanisms.

Representative discussions:
- Community refusal to act as unpaid customer support
- Lost streak and crisis discussions
- Repeated prompts involving deceased friends and damaged homes

## Primary platform and HCI sources

### Android Developers - Mobile UI Design and predictive back
Applied to:
- Android-native navigation and edge-to-edge layout;
- predictive back support;
- adaptive layout and accessibility;
- avoiding a pasted iOS shell.

### Apple Human Interface Guidelines
Applied to:
- native tab, sheet, typography, file-picker and gesture behaviour;
- platform-specific haptics and accessibility;
- consistency with iOS system expectations.

### Research on anthropomorphism and trust in LLM interaction
Applied to:
- warm competence rather than fake intimacy;
- avoiding sycophancy and over-agreement;
- making Melo’s role and uncertainty visible.

### Research on proactive conversational-agent notifications
Applied to:
- user-aware timing;
- aggregation and suppression;
- quiet periods and preference controls.

### Research on progress indicators and perceived waiting
Applied to:
- narrative import progress;
- visible discoveries rather than a dead percentage bar;
- steady progress that communicates real work.

### Self-Determination Theory and personalised gamification research
Applied to:
- autonomy, competence and self-knowledge;
- avoiding coercive rewards, leaderboards and guilt;
- allowing the user to choose Melo’s accountability style.

## Deliberate research stance

No single community is treated as representative of all users. The design direction relies on repeated patterns across multiple products and financial states, then converts them into testable product rules rather than copying feature requests literally.
