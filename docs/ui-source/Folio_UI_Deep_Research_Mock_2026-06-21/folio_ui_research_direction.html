Folio V2 · research-led interface direction

Clean is not empty. Calm is control you can see.

One visual system across iOS and Android, adapted to native behaviour. The interface stays quiet until something matters, makes every automated change traceable, and turns uncertainty into a visible route—not a wall of finance cards.
The signature move: “breathing room” is shown as a living horizon between now and what comes next. Users can inspect, move and test events directly. Melo interprets the route; he never hides it.
