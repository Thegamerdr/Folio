# Privacy And Telemetry Baseline

Financial content is excluded from telemetry by default.

Phase 0 has no runtime telemetry, crash reporting or cloud sync. Future diagnostics must provide preview/redaction and may not include raw financial records unless the user explicitly exports a diagnostic bundle.

All test/demo data must follow `docs/synthetic-data-policy.md`.

## Phase 8 Retention Preferences

Phase 8 retention preference contracts are local product preferences, not telemetry. The synthetic
shell proves that emphasis, celebration intensity, notification frequency and memory depth are
inspectable and resettable, and that hidden sensitive profiling is disabled.

No Phase 8 momentum, ritual or fun-layer record may be exported as analytics by default. Future
diagnostics must summarise preference state without raw financial records, plan titles or sensitive
motivation labels unless the user explicitly previews and exports the bundle.

## Phase 9 Diagnostics And Reviewer Vault

Phase 9 diagnostic contracts are local support evidence, not telemetry. The synthetic shell models
integrity, backup, index and job signals with a sanitised export gate. Diagnostic bundles may not
include raw transactions, merchants, account references, document text, extraction snippets,
financial amounts or user-entered plan labels unless a future release adds an explicit preview and
export consent flow.

The reviewer vault is synthetic, labelled and isolated from user data. It exists to demonstrate
local product flows without real financial records, account creation, cloud sync, Open Banking or
AI routes. It must stay separate from any user vault and must never be merged into telemetry or
production fixtures.

Privacy/data-centre rows must disclose route location, permission state, cloud status, export,
delete and memory reset. A disabled cloud or processor route is not approval to activate it; DPIA
and processor inventory approval remain release blockers before any public beta route changes.

## Phase 10 Cloud Sync And Recovery

Phase 10 cloud/sync contracts are readiness evidence, not telemetry. The synthetic shell models
account, device, recovery, encrypted envelope, snapshot, deletion and beta-gate status without real
account sessions, cloud requests, vault decryption, provider tokens or recovery secrets.

Cloud service metadata is limited to routing and operations fields such as account ID, device ID,
encrypted blob ID, sequence, size, timestamps, entitlement and consent state. Merchant names,
amounts, categories, plan titles, document text, calendar details and account labels must not leave
the encrypted payload.

Diagnostic or support bundles may summarise last backup, active device count, revoked device count,
cursor state and blocker state only after preview and consent. They must not include ciphertext,
plaintext financial content, recovery codes, recovery passphrases, unwrapped keys, provider tokens
or secret fingerprints beyond user-safe public-key identifiers.

Account deletion, cloud disable and recovery verification events are support/audit states, not
behavioural analytics. Any future cloud telemetry requires DPIA, processor inventory approval,
store privacy declaration review and user-facing controls before beta.

## Phase 11 Optional AI

Phase 11 optional-AI contracts are readiness evidence, not telemetry. The synthetic shell models
task schemas, route selection, redacted context, quota, evaluation, consent and beta blockers
without a real provider call, model prompt, account request, network request, database read, user
record or domain write.

Cloud AI inputs must be typed, workspace-scoped and minimised before leaving the device or gateway.
Full account history, raw merchant/account identifiers, SQL, arbitrary tool execution, document
text beyond the selected task scope and unreviewed financial recommendations are not permitted.

AI usage telemetry may record only operational metadata after approval: task type, route, provider
identifier, model identifier, input/output unit counts, weighted units, status, error class and
configured cost units. It must not include prompt text, completion text, raw financial records,
merchant names, account labels, document snippets, plan labels or sensitive user traits.

First cloud-AI use requires task-level explanation, visible quota and provider data-use disclosure.
Denial must keep the local/manual path. Any future cloud AI telemetry, provider retention,
diagnostic upload or evaluation corpus use requires DPIA, processor inventory approval, store
privacy declaration review and user-facing controls before beta.

## Phase 12 Open Banking

Phase 12 Open Banking contracts are readiness evidence, not telemetry. The synthetic shell models
provider selection, consent, dashboard, canonical staging, stale/gap state, revocation and rollout
blockers without a real provider redirect, bank login, network request, account connection, token,
real bank row or transaction write.

Open Banking tokens must be encrypted server-side inside the backend adapter boundary. Provider
tokens, raw account references, refresh tokens, bank credentials and provider secrets must not
appear in the app bundle, mobile local preferences, diagnostic exports, logs or telemetry.

Open Banking operational telemetry, after DPIA and processor approval, may record only consent and
adapter metadata such as consent ID alias, provider ID, status, scope set, selected account aliases,
last successful refresh, stale/gap reason, revoke result, error class and incident correlation ID.
It must not include balances, transaction descriptions, merchant names, raw account identifiers,
provider tokens or user-entered notes.

Revocation, pause, stale-feed, gap-fill and imported-history deletion events are user-control and
support/audit states, not behavioural analytics. Denying or revoking Open Banking must preserve
manual entry and file import paths.

## Phase 13 Business Workspace

Phase 13 business-workspace contracts are readiness evidence, not telemetry. The synthetic shell
models workspace separation, clients, invoices, payment matching, receipts, tax preparation,
calendar items, Melo context, exports, mileage and beta blockers without real business data, client
storage, account creation, native document capture, provider calls, billing entitlement checks,
direct tax filing or beta operations.

Business exports must remain workspace-scoped and traceable. Export metadata may include workspace
alias, period, jurisdiction, currency basis, policy pack version, created timestamp, source evidence
count and unresolved item count. It must not include personal workspace records, personal Melo
memory, personal calendar items or personal document snippets.

Business operational telemetry, after DPIA and processor approval, may record only product
operation metadata such as workspace type, export status, review queue count, blocker class and
support correlation ID. It must not include client names, invoice descriptions, payment references,
document text, mileage routes, tax amounts, merchant names, raw account identifiers or legal/tax
advice text.

Direct filing, public UK tax/MTD claims, accountant collaboration, business billing entitlement,
support workflows and business beta release require tax/legal review, official guidance
verification, accessibility review, support runbook and user-facing controls before launch.

## Phase 14 Store Billing Operations Release

Phase 14 store-release contracts are readiness evidence, not telemetry. The synthetic shell models
store declarations, account deletion, capability entitlements, incident operations, final review,
regression, limited launch, outcome research and roadmap guardrails without real store submission,
billing SDK calls, backend receipt verification, production launch, HMRC filing, household
collaboration or jurisdiction rollout.

Billing and entitlement operational telemetry, after DPIA, processor and store declaration
approval, may record only service metadata such as entitlement capability alias, product alias,
purchase status, restore status, offline grace state, quota counters, incident class and support
correlation ID. It must not include transaction details, account labels, merchant names, document
text, tax values, recovery secrets, provider tokens, raw store receipts or personal financial
content.

Outcome research must remain opt-in or otherwise approved through the privacy programme. It may
measure first-minute value, confidence, corrections, avoidance and plan progress only with data
minimisation and no hidden profiling. Household collaboration, direct HMRC MTD, accountant
collaboration, multiple businesses and additional jurisdictions require separate privacy, threat,
legal and regulatory review before any telemetry or implementation begins.
