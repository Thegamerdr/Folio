# ADR-001: Personal information architecture

Status: Accepted for Phase B.

## Context

Personal Melo needs a small Trusted Core, not every concept as a permanent tab.

## Decision

Personal IA is: Today, Calendar, Review/Activity, Plans, Melo, Trust/Data, Account. Decision History begins as a sub-surface, not a permanent tab.

## Consequences

- Today owns the primary answer.
- Review/Activity owns fact confirmation and recent truth.
- Plans owns scenarios, recovery and payday workbench concepts.
- Trust/Data owns sources, privacy, export, restore and deletion.

## Enforcement

Recorded in `MELO_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `MELO_SCREEN_DISPOSITION.md`.
