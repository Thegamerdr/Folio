# Privacy And Legal Copy Foundation

Date: 2026-06-23

This is not legal advice and is not final legal copy. It is a product-safe foundation for review.

## Principles

- Folio is not financial advice.
- Folio does not make decisions for the user.
- Folio explains consequences and shows what changed.
- The user owns local data.
- Cloud, AI and Open Banking are not required for local mode.
- Local data stays local unless the user chooses otherwise.
- Imports are reviewed before becoming financial reality.
- Rejected evidence does not affect financial reality.
- Melo interprets and explains; Melo does not directly write financial changes.
- Recovery flows must avoid shame, pressure and failure language.

## Must Not Say

- "Folio advises you to..."
- "This is the best decision."
- "Guaranteed."
- "Financial advice."
- "You failed."
- "Your score is..."

## Needs Review Before External Beta

- Privacy policy wording.
- Diagnostic bundle sharing rules.
- Support/contact path.
- Any wording about debt, tax, benefits, affordability or regulated products.
- Store declaration wording.
